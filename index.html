ToolAtlas — Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 July 2026

ToolAtlas helps mechanics keep a record of the tools they own — what they cost, where they live, and the receipts that prove it. That record is yours. This page says plainly what the app stores, what leaves your phone, and what never does.

The short version

  • There is no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no third-party SDK watching what you do. None. The app does not know how often you open it.
  • Signed out, your toolbox never leaves your phone.
  • Signed in, your toolbox is backed up to our database so it survives a lost phone and syncs to your other devices.
  • Browsing the tool catalog sends nothing to us — it is a database stored on your phone.
  • Nothing is ever sold, rented, or shared for advertising.

What stays on your phone

  • The tool catalog. Prices and specifications ship inside the app as a local database. Searching or browsing it makes no network request, so we never see what you looked at.
  • Your toolbox, while signed out. Tools, prices paid, drawers, photos and receipts are held only on the device.
  • Tool images you have chosen to keep. Copies saved for offline use, stored on the device and removable from Profile → Storage.

What we store, if you create an account

An account is optional. The app works fully without one. If you make one, it exists so your records survive losing the phone, and so they appear on your other devices. We store:

  • Your email address and password, handled by our authentication provider. Passwords are hashed — we cannot read yours.
  • Your toolboxes and tools: what you own, quantities, what you paid, purchase dates, notes, categories, and which drawer each lives in.
  • Receipts: vendor, date, total, which tools each covers, and photographs of the paper.
  • Loans: who borrowed a tool, when, and when it is due back.
  • Your details, only if you fill them in: name, business name, email, phone number and postal address. These exist for one reason — they are printed on the insurance claim slips and tax summaries the app generates. They are never used for anything else.

Every row is locked to your account at the database level. Another signed-in user cannot read your records even if they try. Receipt photographs live in private storage and are fetched with short-lived links that only work for you.

What we do not collect

  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind
  • No advertising identifiers, and no advertising
  • No location data
  • No contacts, calendar, or microphone access
  • No device fingerprinting

The app asks for the camera and photo library only when you choose to photograph a tool, a drawer or a receipt, and only uses them for that.

Others who see anything

  • Supabase hosts the database and file storage behind accounts. They process your data on our behalf and do not use it for their own purposes.
  • Tool manufacturers' image servers. Product photographs are loaded from the brands' own websites rather than copied by us, so those servers see an IP address and the image requested — the same as visiting the brand's site. They are not told who you are.
  • Currency rate providers (frankfurter.dev and open.er-api.com), used to convert prices between regions. The app asks only for the day's exchange rates. Nothing about you or your tools is sent.

That is the complete list. Nothing is shared with anyone else, and nothing is sold.

Deleting your account

Profile → Account → Delete my account, inside the app.

It is immediate and permanent: your account, toolboxes, tools, drawers, receipts, receipt photographs and profile details are erased from our servers and cannot be recovered. The copy on your phone stays on your phone — delete the app to remove that too.

You can also just sign out. That leaves your records on the device and stops any syncing.

How long anything is kept

Your records are kept until you delete them or delete your account. There is no other expiry — the point of the app is a record that is still there years later, when an insurer asks for it.

Children

ToolAtlas is meant for working tradespeople and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Your rights

You can see everything we hold, because the app shows it to you. You can edit or delete any of it at any time, and export your records as insurance and tax documents from Profile → Documents. If you are covered by GDPR, UK GDPR or similar law, you also have the right to request a copy of your data or ask us to correct or erase it — email the address below and we will act on it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect or who sees it, the date at the top changes and the app will say so before the change applies.
