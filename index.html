ToolAtlas — Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 August 2026

ToolAtlas keeps a record of the tools you own — what they cost, where they live, and the receipts that prove it. That record is yours. This page says plainly what the app stores, what leaves your phone, and what never does.

The short version

  • There is no advertising, no ad tracking, and no third-party analytics SDK watching what you do.
  • Signed out, your toolbox record never leaves your phone — what you paid, your notes, drawers, photos and receipts stay here.
  • Signed in, your toolbox is backed up so it survives a lost phone and syncs to your other devices.
  • Prices are live now: opening a tool, refreshing your toolbox value, or viewing deals asks our server for current numbers. Those requests carry part numbers — never your notes, prices paid, or photos.
  • Search runs on your phone. The words you type are never sent; the app only asks for current prices of the results on screen.
  • Nothing is ever sold, rented, or shared for advertising.

What stays on your phone

  • The tool catalog itself — every name, part number and photo reference ships in the app, so browsing works offline.
  • Your recent searches and the tools you opened. These fill the Shop tab so it has somewhere to start, they are capped at a couple of dozen, and they are never uploaded — clear them any time from Shop.
  • A short log of changes that added, removed or moved your tools — merges when you signed in, imports, bulk deletes and restores. The last fifty, on this device only, clearable from Profile → Privacy & data. Opening screens is never recorded.
  • Your toolbox record, while signed out — tools, prices paid, drawers, photos and receipts. Price checks send the part numbers being priced and nothing else about them.
  • The last prices the app fetched, kept so your toolbox value still shows offline, labeled with how old it is.
  • Tool images you chose to keep for offline use, removable from Profile → Storage.

What a price check sends

Keeping values current means asking our server for prices. Here is exactly what that involves, because “live prices” should not be a mystery:

  • The part numbers being priced — the tools on the screen, in your box, or on your list.
  • Your account identity if signed in, or your network address if not. Either way it feeds a daily counter used to keep automated scrapers out — the same counter for everyone, used for nothing else.
  • Not sent, ever: what you paid, quantities, notes, drawers, photos, receipts, or the words you typed into search.

Skip it entirely by using the app offline — everything renders from the built-in catalog and the last fetched prices, honestly dated.

What we store, if you create an account

An account is optional and the app works fully without one. It exists so your records survive losing the phone. We store:

  • Your email and password, handled by our authentication provider. Passwords are hashed — we cannot read yours.
  • Your toolboxes and tools: what you own, what you paid, dates, notes and which drawer each lives in.
  • Receipts: vendor, date, total, which tools each covers, and photographs of the paper.
  • Loans: who borrowed a tool and when it is due back.
  • A list of the devices signed in to your account — a name like “iPhone 15”, the platform, and when each last backed up. It exists so you can see where your account is being used and sign the others out. The identifier is generated on the device, is not derived from your phone or from you, and is gone if the app is reinstalled.
  • Your details, only if you fill them in — name, business, phone and address. These exist because they print on your insurance and tax documents, and are used for nothing else.

Every row is locked to your account at the database level. Another signed-in user cannot read your records even if they try. Receipt photographs live in private storage and are fetched with short-lived links that only work for you.

What we do not collect

  • No third-party analytics and no behavioral tracking — the only server-side counting is the daily price-request counter described above
  • No advertising identifiers, and no advertising
  • No location data
  • No contacts, calendar or microphone access

The camera and photo library are used only when you photograph a tool, a drawer or a receipt. If we ever want usage data to build better features, this page changes first and says exactly what and why.

Others who see anything

  • Supabase hosts the database behind accounts and serves the price lookups, on our behalf. They do not use your data for their own purposes.
  • Tool manufacturers' image servers, because product photos load from the brands' own sites rather than being copied by us. They see an IP address and the image asked for — not who you are.
  • Currency rate providers (frankfurter.dev and open.er-api.com), asked only for the day's exchange rates. Nothing about you or your tools is sent.

That is the complete list. Nothing is shared with anyone else, and nothing is sold.

Deleting your account

Profile → Account → Delete my account, inside the app.

It is immediate and permanent: your account, toolboxes, tools, receipts and their photographs are erased from our servers and cannot be recovered. The copy on your phone stays on your phone — delete the app to remove that too.

You can also just sign out. That leaves your records on the device and stops any syncing.

How long anything is kept

Until you delete it. There is no other expiry — the point of the app is a record that is still there years later, when an insurer asks for it.

Children

ToolAtlas is meant for working tradespeople and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Your rights

You can see everything we hold, because the app shows it to you. You can edit or delete any of it at any time, and export your records as insurance and tax documents from Profile → Documents. If you are covered by GDPR, UK GDPR or similar law, you also have the right to request a copy of your data or ask us to correct or erase it — email the address below and we will act on it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect or who sees it, the date at the top changes and the app will say so before the change applies.
